How To Use This App

  1. Mark a bill as paid by changing the status from NO to YES.
  2. The total due automatically subtracts any bill marked YES.
  3. Tithes grow as bills are paid and equal 10% of all paid bills combined.
  4. The date in the main total updates automatically based on today.
  5. Use Reset Month to switch all bills back to NO.
  6. Use Add Bill to create a new bill row without touching formulas.
